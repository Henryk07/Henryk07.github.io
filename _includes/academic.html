About

Hello, I'm Henryk Chan (Haolin Chen), a PhD student in the Department of Engineering at the University of Cambridge. I'm a researcher and medical engineer, and I want the devices I build to make it out of the lab and into clinical use.

My research centres on wearable electrochemical biosensors, wound-monitoring biosensors, and ECG systems built with liquid metal. I've co-authored peer-reviewed papers in SCI Q1 journals, cited {{ site.data.profile.citations_fallback }} times on Google Scholar, and I hold a granted patent for a device design.

Both my BEng and MRes are accredited by the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET).
